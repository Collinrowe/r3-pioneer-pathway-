North Carolina Homeschool Compliance Checklist

Everything the state actually requires, in the order you'll need it. Nothing more.

01

Before You Teach a Single Lesson

  • File a Notice of Intent to Homeschool with the NC Division of Non-Public Education. Due by Aug 1

Starting mid-year? File before instruction begins, rather than waiting for the next Aug 1 deadline.

02

What You're Required to Teach

  • Reading
  • Grammar
  • Spelling
  • Math
  • Geography
  • History of the US & NC
  • Health / Science
03

Testing & Records

  • Administer a nationally standardized test once per year.
  • Keep attendance records for the school year.
  • Keep that year's test results on file.
04

What North Carolina Does Not Require

  • No portfolio review.
  • No curriculum pre-approval.
  • No teaching credential for the instructing parent.
